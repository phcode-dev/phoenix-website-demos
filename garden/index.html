    How to play

    there's no way to lose. wander.

    • drag ↔walk the garden. it never ends in either direction.
    • tap a flowerscatter its petals. it grows back in a few seconds.
    • tap the skya butterfly comes to say hi.
    • ✿ plantgrow a flower of your own. it's still here next time you visit.
    • your flowers ↦walk back to the flowers you planted.
    • ☼ / ☾the sky turns to dusk every minute or so. this makes it happen now.
    • keep walkinga bunny, a scarecrow, a mushroom ring, an old well and signposts are out there. tap them.
    • Mopen this menu. arrows to move, esc to close.
